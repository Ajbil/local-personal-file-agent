# Synthetic Team Handbook

## Incident response

The fictional Atlas support team uses a severity-one incident process. The primary engineer must
acknowledge a page within ten minutes. After acknowledging it, the engineer opens an incident
channel, names an incident commander, and posts a status update every thirty minutes. A written
review is completed within three business days. The review focuses on system improvements rather
than blaming individuals.

## Learning budget

Every fictional Atlas employee receives an annual professional-development allowance of 1,200
credits. The allowance may pay for technical books, online courses, certification examinations,
and engineering conferences. A manager approves requests before purchase. Unused credits expire
at the end of the calendar year and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Office garden

The fictional Atlas office garden contains tomatoes, basil, and yellow marigolds. Volunteers water
the plants on Tuesday and Friday mornings. Gardening tools are stored in a green cabinet beside
the west entrance. Produce from the garden is shared in the office kitchen.
